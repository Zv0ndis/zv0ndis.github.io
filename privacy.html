Privacy Policy

Zvondis School Calendar Sync

What this app is

"Zvondis School Calendar Sync" is a personal utility built for the Semester page at zv0ndis.github.io/semester.html. It keeps school-related events (semester dates, exam periods, calendar entries added on the site) in sync between that page and a Google Calendar belonging to the site owner, Tomáš Zvoníček. It is not a public product — it is used solely by the site owner's own Google account.

What data is accessed

The app requests the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar scope, which allows it to read, create, update, and delete events on the connected Google Calendar. No other Google data (email, contacts, files, etc.) is requested or accessed.

How the data is used

Calendar events are read and written only to keep the website's semester/event data and the connected Google Calendar consistent with each other. Event data (dates and titles) is stored in the site's existing Firebase database and in the connected Google Calendar. No calendar data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Data retention and revocation

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account permissions page. Revoking access immediately stops the sync from reading or writing calendar data.
